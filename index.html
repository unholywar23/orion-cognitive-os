07FAQ

Fair questions.

Does it work offline?

Yes — point Orion at local models and everything runs on your machine. With cloud keys, only the model calls leave your computer; memory and files never do.

Do I need my own AI provider account?

For cloud-grade answers, yes — paste in a key (you pay your provider directly, at cost). Prefer zero cloud? Run local models. Model choice is never tier-gated.

Is Orion a Rewind or Limitless alternative?

Orion isn't a screen recorder — it's a persistent-memory AI assistant. But if what drew you to those products was the local-first promise, Orion actually keeps it: memory lives as files on your own disk, and there's no vendor backend to be acquired or sunset.

What does "screen vision" actually see?

Nothing, until you switch it on. Off by default, visible indicator while watching, one click off. Privacy is enforced in the architecture, not a settings promise.

What am I actually paying for if models are BYOK?

The cognitive OS itself: the memory system, the 277-layer pipeline, the senses, the honesty machinery, and every update as it ships. Your model spend stays between you and your provider — Orion takes nothing off the top.

Refunds?

30-day money-back on every paid tier, no questions. Charter Members keep refund eligibility for life.

What platforms?

Windows today. Memory is plain files, so your data stays portable regardless of what ships next.
